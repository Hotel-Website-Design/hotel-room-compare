# Try Direct Booking Cost Comparison and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for independent hotel owners. Compare the costs in your own booking scenario, using your commission, payment fees and website costs.

[Open the tool](https://hotel-website-design.github.io/hotel-room-compare/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. An illustrative monthly cost comparison with every assumption shown.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This is a simplified cost scenario, not a profit or booking forecast. It excludes tax treatment, refunds, cancellations, marketing, staffing and costs you have not entered. Check how your provider defines commissionable revenue.
